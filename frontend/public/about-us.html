About Us

The Alumni Portal is a shared digital space that helps Tarlac State University alumni stay connected with the university, fellow graduates, and new professional opportunities.

Our Purpose

We aim to strengthen lifelong relationships between graduates and the university. The portal brings essential alumni services into one accessible place, making it easier to maintain professional records, participate in university activities, and discover opportunities for continued growth.

Stay Connected

Reconnect with fellow alumni, receive announcements, participate in events, and remain part of the wider TSU community after graduation.

Build Your Career

Explore employment opportunities, career recommendations, partnerships, and services designed to support alumni throughout their professional journey.

Support Better Programs

Tracer studies and updated employment information help the university understand graduate outcomes and improve academic programs and alumni services.

Engage With the University

Manage appointments, express interest in activities, share feedback, and stay informed about initiatives from the Alumni Affairs Office.

Our Commitment

We are committed to providing an inclusive, useful, and secure online experience for the alumni community. We value accurate information, responsible data handling, and services that create meaningful connections between graduates and Tarlac State University.
