Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Alumni Portal collects, uses, protects, and manages personal data in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012, and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

Effective date: July 18, 2026. By using the Alumni Portal, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where consent is the lawful basis for processing, you may withdraw it subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Information We Collect

  • Identity and contact details, such as name, alumni ID, email address, and telephone number.
  • Academic details, including program, college, year graduated, and other alumni records.
  • Employment, career, tracer-study, event, appointment, and professional information you submit.
  • Account, authentication, security, and technical information needed to operate and protect the portal.

Why We Process Data

  • To verify alumni identity and manage portal accounts.
  • To deliver alumni services, events, appointments, opportunities, and relevant recommendations.
  • To conduct tracer studies, institutional research, reporting, and program improvement.
  • To secure the portal, prevent misuse, comply with legal duties, and respond to authorized requests.

Privacy Principles

Personal data will be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently. Collection will be limited to data that is relevant and necessary for declared, specified, and legitimate purposes. Reasonable organizational, physical, and technical safeguards will be used to protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be accessed by authorized university personnel and approved service providers only when needed for legitimate portal functions. Data may also be disclosed when required by law or a valid order. Personal data will not be sold. Any sharing must observe applicable privacy, confidentiality, and security requirements.

Retention and Disposal

Records will be retained only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose, institutional recordkeeping, or legal obligations. When retention is no longer justified, records will be securely deleted, anonymized, or disposed of according to applicable university policies and law.

Cookies and Technical Data

The portal may use essential browser storage and similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember necessary settings, and protect accounts. Technical logs may be reviewed for reliability, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, and information security.

Your Rights as a Data Subject

Subject to the conditions and limitations provided by law, you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate or incomplete information, erasure or blocking, data portability, and information about how your data is processed. You may object to processing, withdraw consent where consent is relied upon, and lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. You may also seek indemnification when legally warranted.

Requests and Concerns

For access, correction, deletion, objection, or other privacy concerns, contact the Alumni Affairs Office or the university's designated Data Protection Officer through the official university communication channels. A request may require identity verification to protect your account and records.
